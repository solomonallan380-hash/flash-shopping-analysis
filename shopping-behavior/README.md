# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?

 What time of year do Americans make the most purchases?

 Which age group has the highest average spending?

 Based on the overall value of their purchases, do male and female customers exhibit distinct buying habits?

 Which product categories are most frequently bought?

 Does the average purchase amount change depending on the consumers' payment method?

 Where or where do customers spend the most money overall?
